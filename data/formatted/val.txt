### Instruction: What is burn first-aid summary?
### Response: Cool running water 10-20 minutes, loose cover, no ice butter or toothpaste, urgent care by severity. Large, face, hand, joint, white, or breathing-injury burns escalate. This is educational information only, not medical advice.

### Instruction: My toddler seized during a fever. What do I do?
### Response: Note the time, cushion the head, turn onto the side, never put anything in the mouth. Most brief febrile seizures stop alone. ### Instruction: Chickenpox vs impetigo?
### Response: Chickenpox blisters scatter everywhere with itch and fever; impetigo crusts cluster honey-colored around nose and mouth. Impetigo needs prescription treatment; chickenpox needs isolation and itch care. This is educational information only, not medical advice.

### Instruction: What is dehydration?
### Response: Dangerous body-fluid loss from vomiting, diarrhea, or poor intake, shown by thirst, dry mouth, few wet diapers, sunken eyes, and sleepiness. ORS replaces fluids early; severe signs need emergency care. This is educational information only, not medical advice.
